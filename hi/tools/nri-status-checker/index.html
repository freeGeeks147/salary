फुटनोट्स (Footer Notes): 7वां व 8वां वेतन आयोग नियम, DoPT / DoE आदेश, पेंशन और टैक्स सूत्र

[Note 1] 8th CPC Fitment Factor Rule 7 CCS(RP)

Projected 8th CPC Basic Pay is computed as Round(7th CPC Basic × Fitment Factor). The most quoted estimate, 1.92×, came from merging DA of 60% (January 2026) into Basic Pay (1.60×) and adding a 20% real hike; it would raise Level 1 minimum basic pay from ₹18,000 to ₹34,560. DA is 63% from 01.07.2026, so the same 20% hike would now give about 1.96×. The actual factor is not announced yet.

[Note 2] DA Merger & AICPI-IW Jan & Jul Cycle

Dearness Allowance (DA) is revised every 1st January and 1st July based on the 12-month average of the All-India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (AICPI-IW, Base 2016=100) and applies to (Basic Pay + NPA). Upon 8th CPC implementation from 01.01.2026, existing DA merges into the new Basic Pay and resets to 0%.

[Note 3] HRA Slabs & Min Floor DoE OM 2/5/2017

Per DoE OM dated 07.07.2017, HRA is 30% (X), 20% (Y), and 10% (Z) when DA ≥ 50%, subject to a minimum floor of ₹5,400 (X), ₹3,600 (Y), and ₹1,800 (Z) in 7th CPC. When DA resets to 0% in 8th CPC, HRA percentage resets to 24% / 16% / 8% (revising to 27/18/9% at 25% DA and 30/20/10% at 50% DA), with minimum floors scaled proportionally.

[Note 4] Transport Allowance (TA) DoE OM 21/5/2017

Monthly TA is ₹1,350 / ₹900 for Level 1–2 (or ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 if 7th CPC Basic ≥ ₹24,200), ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 for Level 3–8, ₹7,200 / ₹3,600 for Level 9–13A, and ₹15,750 for Level 14+ (Higher TPTA 19 cities vs Other cities), plus DA on TA at the active DA rate. Divyangjan employees receive 2× normal TA.

[Note 5] NPA Ceiling, CGHS & CGEGIS MoHFW / DoE

Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA) for Central Health Service / Govt Doctors is 20% of Basic Pay subject to the statutory ceiling that Basic + NPA ≤ ₹2,37,500 in 7th CPC (scaled in 8th CPC). Mandatory monthly subscriptions: CGHS is ₹250 (L1–5), ₹450 (L6), ₹650 (L7–11), ₹1,000 (L12+); CGEGIS is ₹30 (Group C), ₹60 (Group B), ₹120 (Group A).

[Note 6] UPS, NPS & ₹25L Gratuity DoPPW 2025 Rules

Employee NPS/UPS Tier-I deduction is 10% of (Basic + NPA + DA). Under CCS (Pension) Rules 2021 (Rule 45) & DoPPW OM dated 18.06.2025, Central Govt employees under OPS and UPS qualify for Retirement/Death Gratuity at 1/4th of Emoluments per completed 6-month block (max 16.5×, capped at ₹25,00,000), plus UPS assured 50% pension (at 25+ yrs) and 1/10th lump-sum per 6-month block.

[Note 7] Income Tax FY 2026-27 IT Act 2025

New Tax Regime (Tax Year 2026-27) provides a ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, Employer 14% NPS deduction u/s 80CCD(2), and Section 87A tax rebate up to ₹60,000 (making net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 / gross salary up to ₹12.75L+ completely tax-free, with marginal relief above ₹12L) plus 4% Health & Education Cess.

[Note 8] Arrears & Form 10E Relief Sec 89(1) Relief

Based on the 10-year Pay Commission cycle, the 8th CPC is widely expected to take effect from 01.01.2026 (not yet officially fixed). In 6th & 7th CPC precedents, arrears were paid on revised Basic + DA (less 10% NPS), whereas revised HRA/TA took effect from the date of notification. Lump-sum arrears qualify for retrospective slab relief under Section 89(1) (Form 10E).
