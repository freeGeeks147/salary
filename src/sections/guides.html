📰 Government Employee News Adda (8th CPC & DA Updates)

Oct 2026
1. DA Raised to 63% from July 2026 (approved 28 Oct 2026)

The Union Cabinet approved a 3-point increase in DA and DR, from 60% to 63% of basic pay and pension, with effect from 1 July 2026; arrears from July are due. The 8th CPC fitment factor is not announced yet; the staff side (NC-JCM) has asked for 3.833.

2. Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) vs NPS Choice Window

Central Government employees with 25+ years qualifying service are guaranteed 50% of 12-month average Basic Pay as assured pension plus Dearness Relief (DR) and a lump-sum payout (1/10th per 6 months).

3. New Income Tax Act Forms (Effective FY 2026-27)

Zero tax up to ₹12,00,000 taxable income (₹12.75 Lakh gross for salaried with ₹75,000 Standard Deduction). Old Form 16 is now Form 130; Form 12BB is Form 124; Form 26AS is Form 168.

📚 CAPF Handbook, Railway Hub & CPC Glossary

Reference Guide
🎖️ CAPF / Paramilitary (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles): Constable (GD) starts at Level 3 (₹21,700), Head Constable at Level 4 (₹25,500), SI (GD) at Level 6 (₹35,400), and Assistant Commandant (UPSC CAPF) at Level 10 (₹56,100) + Risk & Hardship Allowance (₹6,000–₹25,000/mo), Ration Money Allowance (₹3,965/mo), and Dress Allowance.
🚆 Indian Railways Salary & Allowances Hub: Track Maintainer (Group D) starts at Level 1 (₹18,000 + ₹2,700 Risk Allow.), Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) at Level 2 (₹19,900 + 30% Pay Element Running Allowance), Station Master / JE at Level 6 (₹35,400), plus 78-Day Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB ₹17,951) and Night Duty Allowance.
📖 Key CPC Glossary (Fitment, DNI, MACP, NPA, CTG): Fitment Factor: Multiplier applied to 7th CPC Basic Pay to arrive at 8th CPC Basic Pay. DNI: Date of Next Increment (1st Jan or 1st Jul). MACP: Assured financial upgradation at 10, 20 & 30 years of service. CTG: Composite Transfer Grant (80% of Basic Pay).
