UPS vs NPS vs OPS: pension, gratuity and leave encashment

Central government employees who joined on or after 1 January 2004 are covered by the National Pension System (NPS), and from 1 April 2025 can opt for the Unified Pension Scheme (UPS), which offers an assured pension of 50% of the average basic pay of the last 12 months after 25 years of qualifying service. Employees who joined earlier remain under the Old Pension Scheme (OPS).

Enter your qualifying service, earned leave balance and pay basis (7th or projected 8th CPC) to compare your estimated monthly pension under UPS and NPS, along with retirement gratuity, leave encashment (up to 300 days) and OPS commutation.

The MACP / promotion pay fixation calculator applies FR 22(I)(a)(1): one notional increment in your current level, then placement in the next-higher cell of the new level.
