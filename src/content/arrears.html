8th CPC arrears, DA arrears and allowance calculators

The 8th Pay Commission is expected to take effect from 1 January 2026, ten years after the 7th CPC. If it is implemented later, employees are usually paid arrears: the difference between revised and old pay for every month since the effective date. Enter the number of months and the month of your annual increment to estimate your total 8th CPC arrears, including HRA arrears if you choose.

This page also includes a DA arrears calculator for the months between a DA hike being due and being paid, a Section 10(13A) HRA exemption calculator for the old tax regime, a night duty allowance (NDA) calculator, and a Diwali bonus calculator for productivity-linked (PLB) and ad-hoc bonus.

Arrears and bonus figures are estimates; the final amounts depend on the orders issued by your ministry or department.
