Calculators for every part of a government employee's pay and service

Beyond basic salary, central government pay depends on many allowances and service rules. This collection of 94+ calculators covers DA and HRA, transport allowance, LTC, TA/DA on tour and transfer, special posting allowances for the North East, Ladakh and islands, CGHS contribution and ward entitlement, leave rules (EL, HPL, CCL, maternity and paternity leave), subsistence allowance, government quarters and more.

Every tool uses the pay level and basic pay you set in the main calculator, and can switch between 7th CPC pay and projected 8th CPC pay. Each result lists the rule or office memorandum it is based on.
