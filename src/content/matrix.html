About the 7th CPC pay matrix and the projected 8th CPC matrix

The 7th Central Pay Commission replaced pay bands and grade pay with a single pay matrix of 18 pay levels (19 including Level 13A). Each level has a vertical ladder of cells, and every annual increment moves you one cell down, an increase of about 3%. Level 1 starts at ₹18,000 and Level 18 (Cabinet Secretary) is fixed at ₹2,50,000.

This table shows every cell of the 7th CPC matrix next to the projected 8th CPC basic pay at the fitment factor you choose. Click any cell to load that exact basic pay into the salary calculator and see your full in-hand pay.
