8th Pay Commission: latest status and what to expect

The Government of India announced the 8th Central Pay Commission on 16 January 2025. Its Terms of Reference were notified in November 2025, with Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai as Chairperson. The new pay structure is expected to be effective from 1 January 2026, and arrears are expected from that date if implementation comes later.

The most discussed number is the fitment factor, the multiplier that converts 7th CPC basic pay into 8th CPC basic pay. A 1.92× factor would raise the minimum basic pay from ₹18,000 to about ₹34,560. The final factor will be decided by the Commission and the Government.
