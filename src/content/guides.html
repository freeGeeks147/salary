8th Pay Commission: latest status and what to expect

The Government of India announced the 8th Central Pay Commission on 16 January 2025, the Cabinet approved its Terms of Reference on 28 October 2025, and it was constituted on 3 November 2025 with Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai as Chairperson. Its report is due within 18 months, by about May 2027. The effective date is not officially fixed, but the new pay structure is widely expected to count from 1 January 2026, with arrears if implementation comes later.

The most discussed number is the fitment factor, the multiplier that converts 7th CPC basic pay into 8th CPC basic pay. A 1.92× factor would raise the minimum basic pay from ₹18,000 to about ₹34,560. The final factor will be decided by the Commission and the Government.
