Income tax for government employees, FY 2026-27

This calculator works out the income tax on your salary under both the new tax regime and the old tax regime, and tells you which one saves you more. Under the new regime, salaried employees get a ₹75,000 standard deduction and the Section 87A rebate makes taxable income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free, with marginal relief just above that limit. Employer NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2) is deductible in both regimes.

Under the old regime you can also claim Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh), Section 80D health insurance, and HRA exemption under Section 10(13A). Health and education cess of 4% is added in both regimes.

The one-tap presets load typical pay for common posts in Railways, Defence, CAPF, SSC and medical services into the salary calculator.
