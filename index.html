How the 8th Pay Commission salary calculator works

This calculator compares your current 7th CPC in-hand salary with your projected 8th Pay Commission salary, side by side. Pick your pay level and cell from the official 7th CPC pay matrix, choose a fitment factor, select your city class and DA rate, and every component is recalculated instantly: basic pay, dearness allowance (DA), house rent allowance (HRA), transport allowance (TA) with DA on TA, NPS contribution, CGHS, professional deductions and income tax (TDS).

Your projected 8th CPC basic pay is your current 7th CPC basic pay multiplied by the fitment factor. The default 1.92× fitment factor reflects the widely discussed estimate: DA of about 60% merged into basic pay (1.60×) plus a real increase of about 20%. Move the slider anywhere from 1.83× to 2.86× to see other scenarios.

All 8th Pay Commission figures are estimates for planning until the Commission's recommendations are accepted and notified by the Government of India. 7th CPC figures follow the current DoPT, Department of Expenditure and CBDT rules.

Frequently asked questions

How is 8th Pay Commission salary calculated?

8th CPC basic pay is your current 7th CPC basic pay multiplied by the fitment factor. DA, HRA and transport allowance are then calculated on the new basic pay, and NPS, CGHS and income tax are deducted to get your in-hand salary.

What is the expected fitment factor for the 8th Pay Commission?

The fitment factor has not been announced yet. Estimates range from about 1.83× to 2.86×; this calculator uses 1.92× by default, which reflects DA of about 60% merged into basic pay plus a real increase of about 20%.

What will the minimum basic pay be under the 8th CPC?

At a 1.92× fitment factor, the minimum basic pay of ₹18,000 would rise to about ₹34,560. At 2.57× (the 7th CPC factor) it would be ₹46,260. The final figure depends on the fitment factor the Government approves.

When will the 8th Pay Commission be implemented?

The 8th CPC is expected to be effective from 1 January 2026. If it is implemented later, arrears are expected to be paid from that date.

Is this salary calculator free?

Yes. All calculators on 8thCPCPay.com are free and run entirely in your browser; your inputs are not sent anywhere.

📌 Official Calculation Methodology & Statutory Footnotes

Footer Notes: 7th & 8th CPC Rules, DoPT / DoE OMs, Pension & Tax Formulas

[Note 1] 8th CPC Fitment Factor Rule 7 CCS(RP)

Projected 8th CPC Basic Pay is computed as Round(7th CPC Basic × Fitment Factor). With Dearness Allowance reaching 60% on 01.01.2026, merging DA into Basic Pay yields a base factor of 1.60×; adding a 20% real pay hike produces the consensus 1.92× fitment factor (raising Level 1 minimum basic pay from ₹18,000 to ₹34,560).

[Note 2] DA Merger & AICPI-IW Jan & Jul Cycle

Dearness Allowance (DA) is revised every 1st January and 1st July based on the 12-month average of the All-India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (AICPI-IW, Base 2016=100) and applies to (Basic Pay + NPA). Upon 8th CPC implementation from 01.01.2026, existing DA merges into the new Basic Pay and resets to 0%.

[Note 3] HRA Slabs & Min Floor DoE OM 2/5/2017

Per DoE OM dated 07.07.2017, HRA is 30% (X), 20% (Y), and 10% (Z) when DA ≥ 50%, subject to a minimum floor of ₹5,400 (X), ₹3,600 (Y), and ₹1,800 (Z) in 7th CPC. When DA resets to 0% in 8th CPC, HRA percentage resets to 24% / 16% / 8% (revising to 27/18/9% at 25% DA and 30/20/10% at 50% DA), with minimum floors scaled proportionally.

[Note 4] Transport Allowance (TA) DoE OM 21/5/2017

Monthly TA is ₹1,350 / ₹900 for Level 1–2 (or ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 if 7th CPC Basic ≥ ₹24,200), ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 for Level 3–8, ₹7,200 / ₹3,600 for Level 9–13A, and ₹15,750 for Level 14+ (Higher TPTA 19 cities vs Other cities), plus DA on TA at the active DA rate. Divyangjan employees receive 2× normal TA.

[Note 5] NPA Ceiling, CGHS & CGEGIS MoHFW / DoE

Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA) for Central Health Service / Govt Doctors is 20% of Basic Pay subject to the statutory ceiling that Basic + NPA ≤ ₹2,37,500 in 7th CPC (scaled in 8th CPC). Mandatory monthly subscriptions: CGHS is ₹250 (L1–5), ₹450 (L6), ₹650 (L7–11), ₹1,000 (L12+); CGEGIS is ₹30 (Group C), ₹60 (Group B), ₹120 (Group A).

[Note 6] UPS, NPS & ₹25L Gratuity DoPPW 2025 Rules

Employee NPS/UPS Tier-I deduction is 10% of (Basic + NPA + DA). Under CCS (Pension) Rules 2021 (Rule 45) & DoPPW OM dated 18.06.2025, Central Govt employees under OPS and UPS qualify for Retirement/Death Gratuity at 1/4th of Emoluments per completed 6-month block (max 16.5×, capped at ₹25,00,000), plus UPS assured 50% pension (at 25+ yrs) and 1/10th lump-sum per 6-month block.

[Note 7] Income Tax FY 2026-27 IT Act 2025

New Tax Regime (Tax Year 2026-27) provides a ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, Employer 14% NPS deduction u/s 80CCD(2), and Section 87A tax rebate up to ₹60,000 (making net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 / gross salary up to ₹12.75L+ completely tax-free, with marginal relief above ₹12L) plus 4% Health & Education Cess.

[Note 8] Arrears & Form 10E Relief Sec 89(1) Relief

Based on the 10-year Pay Commission cycle, the 8th CPC is widely expected to take effect from 01.01.2026 (not yet officially fixed). In 6th & 7th CPC precedents, arrears were paid on revised Basic + DA (less 10% NPS), whereas revised HRA/TA took effect from the date of notification. Lump-sum arrears qualify for retrospective slab relief under Section 89(1) (Form 10E).
